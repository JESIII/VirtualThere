# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Our standard

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of conduct. The full text -
including the standards of behaviour, the responsibilities of maintainers, the scope, and the
enforcement guidelines with their four-step escalation ladder - is available at:

* https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
* Plain text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/code_of_conduct.txt

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, criticise the code and not the person,
and keep technical disagreement technical. Behaviour that the Contributor Covenant treats as
unacceptable is unacceptable here too.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and
code comments - and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private
channel:

* open a **private security advisory** on the repository (Security -> Advisories -> "Report a
  vulnerability"), which is private to maintainers, and mark it as a conduct report; or
* contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
